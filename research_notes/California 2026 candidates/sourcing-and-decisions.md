## Editorial rules applied

- Candidates in each race are listed A to Z by last name, not by primary finish.
- Every "What they've done" box links to sources. Plans come from campaign sites, the official Voter Information Guide statements (https://vig.cdn.sos.ca.gov/2026/general/pdf/candidate-statements.pdf) and news coverage.
- Record labels describe facts, not judgments:
  - **Has a record**: acted on the issue in a past job.
  - **Changed position**: a documented change from an earlier stated or acted position.
  - **No record yet**: has never held a job where they could act on the issue.
  - **Facts disputed**: credible sources or the two sides disagree.
  - **Not reviewed yet**: a record likely exists but was not checked. This label currently appears only on Becerra's congressional tax votes.
- Self-reported numbers are labeled as self-reported. These are Bonta's lawsuit counts and Ma's housing-unit counts.
- Only independent pollsters are shown:
  - Governor: Emerson, Berkeley IGS and PPIC.
  - Insurance Commissioner: Probolsky, which states it has no client in the race.

## Left out on purpose (unverified or unreliable)

- **Hilton** calling deportations "perfect": CapRadio's two pieces conflict. Mentioned only as disputed.
- **Becerra** backing out of the LA Times and Univision debates: sourced mainly from partisan outlets.
- **Cohen** undercover-video audit claims: these came from O'Keefe Media and the California Globe.
- **Controller poll** (Wolfson memo): the sponsor is unknown, so it was not used.
- **Secretary of State poll** (Independent Voter Project): IVP is an advocacy group, and the topline numbers are not in its article.
- **Allen** returning insurance-industry donations: paywalled (Politico Pro) and unverified.
- **Hawks** running for governor in 2023: the only source is an AI aggregator.
- **Wagner**:
  - The 2021 vaccine "tracking devices" question was left out because it is not about the office's duties.
  - His defense of Andrew Do was left out for the same reason.
- **Romero** radio host claim: unverified.
- **Fundraising**:
  - Cal-Access blocked automated access, so totals come from news reports with as-of dates.
  - Weber and Wagner totals came only from an unknown blog, so the page says they could not be confirmed.

## Santa Clara County local races (added Oct 21, 2026)

Districts on the ballot come from the certified June 2 primary results:
- U.S. House: `76-us-rep.pdf`
- State Senate: `90-state-senator.pdf`
- State Assembly: `95-state-assembly.pdf`

Candidate names and ballot designations come from the certified November list.

- **U.S. House:**
  - Districts: CD16 (Liccardo v. Soulé), CD17 (Khanna v. Tandon), CD18 (Lofgren v. Lewis), CD19 (Panetta v. Verbica).
  - Prop 50 maps changed the Santa Clara County lines only slightly (San Jose Spotlight).
  - House votes come from clerk.house.gov roll calls, and money comes from FEC.
- **State Senate:**
  - SD10 (Sakakihara v. Price) is the only even-numbered Senate district in the county, and the seat is empty since Wahab went to Congress.
  - SD13 and SD15 are not up this year.
- **State Assembly:** AD23, 24, 25, 26, 28 and 29. Incumbent bill records come from CalMatters Digital Democracy and the members' own pages, which list mostly wins. Vetoes and failures are included where found.
- **County offices:**
  - No Board of Supervisors, DA, Sheriff, Assessor or judge contests are on the November ballot; all were decided in June.
  - Source: Registrar data reposted by Local News Matters. vote.santaclaracounty.gov blocked automated access.
- **San Jose City Council:** runoffs only in Districts 5, 7 and 9. San Jose has no city measure in November; the hotel tax was on the June ballot.
- **Measures:**
  - The Regional Transit Measure (0.5% sales tax, 14 years) is countywide.
  - The vote threshold was confirmed by only one source, so the page does not state it.
  - School parcel tax and bond measures (O, P, Q, R) are mentioned only as "check your sample ballot."
- **District areas:** the areas shown are approximate city lists. The page tells voters to confirm their districts with My Voter Status or their sample ballot.

Left out on purpose:
- Sealed, unverified group-text allegations about Ortiz.
- Pending defamation suits and counter-allegations between Doan and Le, which are civil claims with no findings.
- Khanna's stock-trade count (Washington Examiner only).
- Tandon's claim about undocumented residents (unsupported).
- Campaign claims about opponents.

Open items:
- Money totals for most Assembly candidates could not be confirmed (Cal-Access blocked).
- No local debates or polls were found.
