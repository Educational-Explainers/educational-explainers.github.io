## Remaining limits and follow-up

The source inventory contains 277 unique URLs, including county-office links. The automated fetch retrieved 209; 68 failed due to access restrictions, certificate errors, missing pages or other fetch errors. Some were subsequently accessible through web search. A successful fetch is not proof of a claim; a failed fetch is not proof a claim is false.

Not independently certified in this pass: every endorsement and fundraising total; every campaign platform or biography detail; school achievement statistics and attribution of gains; every state bill's final text, effective date and implementation; all court-case procedural histories; all municipal policy votes; all county-to-district mappings; local primary percentages; the Berkeley and insurance polls. Existing dated claims remain source-attributed rather than newly certified. Some historical claims are supported by news reporting rather than primary records.

Specific follow-ups: Becerra's tax votes; Morgan's pension-board decisions; Yemen resolution H.Con.Res.124's latest actions; SB 1301's enacted notice requirements; AB 181's provision-specific dates; updated financial disclosures. The original research notes list additional gaps. Several Treasurer links returned 404 and should be replaced with the matching archived records before treating their numerical claims as independently verified.

The guide now links this review and avoids claiming that every included statement has been independently confirmed. Prominent reciprocal navigation was added at the top of both election guides.
